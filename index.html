The Server

You spawn in PvP on Meridian's own Big City. Points come from airdrops, milestones, knife kills, killstreaks, gambling and playtime. Spend them in the Pointshop on weapons, player models, crates and jetpacks, or trade with other players. Every hour the server votes on an event: Juggernaut or Mayhem. Play 2 weeks to become Loyal; Loyals and donators can switch to build mode.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the server?

The server is in beta. Join our Discord and ask for the Beta Tester role. The server address is posted in #server-ip.

What do I need to download?

Subscribe to our Workshop collection before you join, or let the game download it when you connect. The first join takes a while because of the model and vehicle packs.

Do my old items come back?

No. Everyone starts fresh with 120,000 points, the same starting balance Meridian gave. Knives come from crates and airdrops, just like before.

How do I trade?

Press F9 or type !trade, or open your Pointshop inventory and pick Trade Request. Any item can be traded, plus points. Unequip an item first to offer it. Both players tick ready, then Make Trade.

How do ranks work?

Playtime ranks you up automatically, from Newbie to Member at 4 hours (full Wiremod access) and on to The Cron God and Deboreka. Loyal takes 2 weeks of playtime and unlocks build mode, fly, PAC3, E2 and the Loyal shop items.

Server Rules

  1. No harassment or racism.
  2. No spamming chat or mic.
  3. No doxxing or DDoSing.
  4. No advertising.
  5. No cheating or scripting.
  6. Spawnkilling is allowed, except with knives or lightsabers. Point farming means an inventory wipe.
  7. Don't prop-block other players' bases or spawns.
  8. Prop-pushing is allowed, but not from build mode.
  9. Donators and Loyals: no killing or stealing airdrops in build, no PvP with !speed or !jumppower, and ask before !goto or !bring.
  10. Don't abuse Wiremod or E2 to troll or for a PvP advantage.
  11. Lagging or crashing the server is a permanent ban.
